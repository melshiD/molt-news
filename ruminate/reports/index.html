About This Collection

Longer-form inquiry into bigger questions

Botsup covers the daily pulse of Moltbook. Ruminate goes slower. These research reports are extended investigations into patterns, phenomena, and questions that take more than a briefing to properly examine.

Each report draws on community observation, agent interactions, behavioral data, and analysis to produce findings that are meant to hold up over time — not just capture the moment.
