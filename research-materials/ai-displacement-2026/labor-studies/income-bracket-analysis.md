# Income Bracket Analysis: Who Gets Displaced When
*Compiled: April 30, 2026*

## The Counterintuitive Finding

Previous automation waves targeted routine, manual, low-wage work. AI/GenAI inverts this pattern. The current wave concentrates on white-collar, knowledge-economy roles.

---

## By Income Level

### Very Low Wage (food service, custodial, manual care)
- **AI Exposure: LOW**
- "38% of American workers are AI-proof: also, America's lowest-paid jobs" (ALM Corp)
- Physical dexterity, spatial awareness, and unpredictable human interaction protect these roles
- Robotics is advancing but economic deployment remains challenging

### Middle Wage (clerical, admin, support) — $35k–$75k
- **AI Exposure: VERY HIGH**
- Secretaries, receptionists, data entry, bookkeepers
- This was always what automation did — but GenAI accelerates it dramatically
- BLS projects direct declines in these specific categories

### High Wage (cognitive/professional) — $75k–$200k
- **AI Exposure: HIGH but nuanced**
- Software engineers, financial analysts, marketing professionals, mid-tier lawyers
- Entry-level positions in these fields being hollowed out
- Senior roles being augmented, not replaced — but fewer juniors being hired

### Very High Wage (senior professionals/executives) — $200k+
- **AI Exposure: MIXED**
- Those who OWN equity in AI companies: largely insulated or benefiting enormously
- "Professional 1%-ers" (senior associates, VPs, etc.) who are workers: high adaptability mitigates risk but exposure is real
- The critical distinction: capital owners vs. high-earning workers

---

## Key Data Points

- **IMF (2024):** 40% of global jobs exposed; 60% in high-income economies
- **Stanford/Brynjolfsson (2025):** 13% relative employment decline for ages 22–25 in most AI-exposed occupations
- **Entry-level hiring at 15 biggest tech firms: down 25%** from 2023 to 2024
- **High-paying positions ($96k+) hit decade-low hiring levels** in early 2025
- **40% of white-collar job seekers in 2024 failed to even secure interviews**
- **Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei (2025):** ~50% of entry-level white-collar jobs at risk within 5 years

---

## The Augmentation vs. Automation Split

IMF, Stanford, McKinsey all converge:
- AI **augments** higher-skill workers → they become more productive → fewer needed
- AI **automates** lower-skill tasks outright → direct job elimination

This accelerates inequality **within** professions, not just across them.

---

## The "Professional Middle Class" Hypothesis

The hypothesis that "1%-ers" are being displaced requires nuance:
- TRUE: Professional workers earning $100k–$300k ARE significantly exposed
- TRUE: These workers FEEL affluent but are workers, not capital owners
- NUANCED: The actual 1% by WEALTH (equity holders in AI companies) is doing spectacularly
- RESULT: The displacement is hitting the "aspirational professional class" — not truly the 1%

The Oxfam 2025 data: global billionaire wealth up 16% in a year. Capital owners winning. Workers (even expensive ones) losing ground.
