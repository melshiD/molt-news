# AI Displacement Waves: Research Notes
*Compiled: April 30, 2026*

## Overview
Research sources converge on a three-wave model of AI/automation displacement, with the critical inversion that cognitive/white-collar work is hit first — the opposite of all prior automation waves.

---

## Wave 1 (2023–2027): Cognitive Routine Tasks — ALREADY UNDERWAY

**Jobs most affected first:**
- Customer service representatives (~80% automation potential)
- Data entry clerks (7.5M positions at risk by 2027)
- Administrative assistants and executive secretaries
- Translators and content writers (contractor roles first)
- Entry-level software engineering
- Paralegals, legal assistants, legal researchers
- Financial clerks and credit authorizers

**Key data:**
- WEF Future of Jobs Report 2025: fastest-declining roles are clerical/secretarial workers
- BLS projects declining demand for procurement clerks, credit authorizers, customer service reps
- Bank tellers: -15% by 2033 (51,400 jobs)
- Cashiers: -11% by 2033 (353,100 jobs)

---

## Wave 2 (2026–2029): Agentic AI — Whole Workflow Automation

**The 2026-2027 "transition phase"** — GenAI evolves into autonomous agentic AI capable of multi-step workflows, not just individual tasks.

**Jobs facing second-wave pressure:**
- Mid-level financial analysts
- Mid-tier software engineers (not just entry-level)
- Business consultants for standardized engagements
- Radiologists and diagnostic imaging (though current data defies this prediction)
- Accountants handling standard reporting
- Recruiting and HR professionals
- Sales development representatives

**Key data:**
- PwC: agent-exposed roles facing 66% skill obsolescence
- Goldman Sachs: agents could replace 7% of US roles by 2029
- 40% of services like customer support reaching full automation in this phase

---

## Wave 3 (2029–2035): Physical-World and Complex Judgment Roles

**Jobs facing later pressure:**
- Skilled trades (physical + cognitive complexity)
- Senior knowledge workers in law, medicine (judgment-based)
- Teaching and education
- Social work and counseling
- Creative and strategic roles

**McKinsey timeline acceleration:**
- Original projection: 50% of work hours automatable by 2053
- Updated (post-GenAI): midpoint moved to **2045** — a decade earlier

---

## Aggregate Estimates (Major Institutions)

| Source | Year | Estimate |
|---|---|---|
| Goldman Sachs | 2023/2025 | 300M jobs globally affected; 6–7% US actual displacement |
| IMF | Jan 2024 | 40% of global jobs exposed; 60% in advanced economies |
| WEF | Jan 2025 | 92M displaced, 170M created by 2030; net +78M |
| McKinsey | 2023 | 30% of US work hours automatable by 2030 |
| Stanford/Brynjolfsson | Aug 2025 | 13% relative decline in AI-exposed entry-level employment |
| BLS | 2025 | Net 3.1% employment growth 2024–2034 (aggregate stable) |

---

## Critical Distinction
Oxford's 2013 Frey & Osborne study (47% of US jobs at risk) predicted LOW-income, routine jobs most at risk. GenAI has partially **inverted** this: higher-income knowledge-based work is now the front line of exposure.
