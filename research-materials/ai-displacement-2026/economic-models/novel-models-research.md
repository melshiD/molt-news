# Novel Economic Models for Post-Displacement Society
*Compiled: April 30, 2026*

## 1. Universal Basic Income (UBI)

### Key Pilots and Results

**Kenya — GiveDirectly (2017–ongoing)**
- ~23,000 recipients, 195 villages
- Long-term payments (~$0.75/adult/day for 12 years)
- Results: 23% increase in average consumption
- Recipients shifted to self-employment; entrepreneurship rose
- Domestic violence fell; school attendance improved
- "Laziness effect" did NOT materialize
- Source: GiveDirectly | https://www.givedirectly.org/2023-ubi-results

**Finland (2017–2018)**
- €560/month to 2,000 unemployed (age 25–58) for 2 years
- Employment: no significant difference vs. control
- Well-being: higher life satisfaction (7.32 vs 6.76/10), better health (54.8% vs 46.2%)
- Less stress, more confidence

**Stockton, CA — SEED (2019–2021)**
- $500/month to 125 residents for 24 months
- Recipients found full-time employment at DOUBLE the rate of non-recipients
- Improved emotional health, reduced income volatility

### Costs and Concerns
- US-wide $1,000/month per adult: ~$3 trillion/year
- Critics: could reduce pressure for structural reform
- Pilots too small/short to capture macro effects (inflation, equilibrium shifts)

## 2. Universal Basic Services (UBS)

Proposed by Anna Coote and Andrew Percy (New Economics Foundation / UCL):
- Collectively-provided FREE services: healthcare, housing, education, transport, digital access
- Cheaper than UBI (services at collective cost, not individual cash equivalent)
- Addresses systematic market failures regardless of income
- Keeps workers free to take risks (gig, startups, caregiving) without catastrophic downside

Source: LSE Public Policy Review | https://ppr.lse.ac.uk/articles/10.31389/lseppr.12

## 3. Platform Cooperatives (Worker-Owned AI)

**Mondragon Corporation (Spain)**
- 80,000+ worker-owners, 100+ cooperatives
- Income ratio capped at 6:1 (vs US avg 344:1)
- Living wages, local profit reinvestment
- Source: CSMonitor, 2024 | https://www.csmonitor.com/Business/2024/0513/income-inequality-capitalism-mondragon-corporation

**Stocksy United**
- Artist-owned stock photo cooperative
- ~1,000 contributing artists
- $10.7M annual revenue, ~95% goes to artists

**Key advantage in AI context:** If workers own the platform and AI tools, productivity gains from AI flow to LABOR not external shareholders.

**Limitations:** Struggle to scale at VC-funded pace; financing challenges since equity investment is structurally incompatible with worker ownership.

## 4. Robot Taxes

**Bill Gates (2017):** Tax companies deploying automation at rate equivalent to displaced workers' income taxes. Revenue → retraining and social programs.

**EU 2017:** Considered and rejected (feared innovation slowdown).

**Bernie Sanders (October 2025):** HELP Committee report "The Big Tech Oligarchs' War Against Workers":
- Robot tax on corporations for each automated position
- Mandatory 32-hour workweek
- 45% worker representation on corporate boards
- Employee ownership requirements
- Estimated: AI could eliminate ~100M US jobs in next decade

**OpenAI (April 2026):** Endorsed robot tax in "Industrial Policy for the Intelligence Age":
- Levy on automated labor replacing human workers
- Shift tax base from payroll → capital gains and corporate income
Source: TechCrunch | https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/06/openais-vision-for-the-ai-economy-public-wealth-funds-robot-taxes-and-a-four-day-work-week/

**Challenges:** Hard to define "robot"; potential consumer price pass-through; international competitiveness concerns.

## 5. Sovereign Wealth Funds from AI Gains

**OpenAI (April 2026):** Proposed nationally managed public wealth fund, partially seeded by AI companies, invested long-term, returns distributed to citizens.

**Model:** Alaska Permanent Fund — oil revenue dividends paid to every state resident since 1982.

**Convergence Analysis "Lead, Own, Share" framework:** Sovereign wealth funds should acquire equity stakes directly in AI infrastructure — citizens become capital owners, not just redistribution recipients.

**Current momentum:** UK launched £500M Sovereign AI Unit; Saudi Arabia's PIF formed Humain; Abu Dhabi wealth funds investing in AI.

Source: Convergence Analysis | https://www.convergenceanalysis.org/fellowships/economics/lead-own-share-sovereign-wealth-funds-for-transformative-ai

## 6. Data Dividends / Personal Data as Labor

**California Gov. Newsom (2019):** "Data dividend" — compensation from companies profiting from personal data.

**Andrew Yang 2020:** Data as property; tech companies pay users. Now runs Data Dividend Project using CCPA for collective bargaining.

**Theoretical basis:** AI models trained on human-generated data (text, images, behavior). If data = labor, contributors deserve a wage.

**Critical limitation:** Individual data has near-zero marginal value. Only aggregated data at scale creates AI model value → per-user payments vanishingly small.

**EFF criticism:** Risks legitimizing data collection rather than demanding stronger privacy.

## 7. Decentralized Autonomous Organizations (DAOs)

**Current state (2024):**
- 13,000+ DAOs globally
- $24.5B combined treasury
- 11.1M governance token holders
- Market: $170M (2024) → projected $333M by 2031

**Theory in AI context:** DAO-governed AI product → productivity gains flow to token holders (workers, users) not concentrated shareholders.

**Limitations:** Governance capture by large holders; technical exclusivity; legal ambiguity; poor security track record.
