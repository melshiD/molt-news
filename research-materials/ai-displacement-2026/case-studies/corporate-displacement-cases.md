# Corporate AI Displacement: Documented Case Studies
*Compiled: April 30, 2026*

---

## IBM — Back-Office Hiring Freeze (2023)
- CEO Arvind Krishna: paused hiring for roles AI could replace
- Projected ~7,800 of 26,000 back-office roles replaceable within 5 years
- Roles: HR, accounting, administrative
- Impact: AskHR chatbot replaced hundreds of HR positions

**Source:** Bloomberg, May 2023 | https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2023-05-01/ibm-to-pause-hiring-for-back-office-jobs-that-ai-could-kill

## Duolingo — Contractor Wave (January 2024)
- Cut ~10% of contractor workforce
- AI handles translation and content generation tasks
- CEO declared company "AI-first": headcount only grows if teams can't automate more

**Source:** TechCrunch, Jan 2024 | https://techcrunch.com/2024/01/09/duolingo-cut-10-of-its-contractor-workforce-as-the-company-embraces-ai/

## Chegg — Structural Collapse (2024–2025)
- Cut 22% of staff (~240 jobs) as students switched to free ChatGPT
- October 2025: additional 45% cut (~388 employees)
- This is AI destroying demand for a business, not just replacing workers

## Klarna — The 700-Agent Experiment and Reversal
**The experiment:** AI assistant handled 75% of customer chats (2.3M conversations); performed work of 700 agents; projected $40M annual savings.

**The reversal:** CEO admitted it "negatively affected service and product quality." Customer satisfaction dropped. Klarna rehired human agents (calling it "VIP experience").

**The lesson:** AI CAN replace the volume but fails on complex, emotionally charged interactions. Quality-sensitive deployments face real limits.

**Source:** CNBC, Fast Company | https://www.fastcompany.com/91468582/klarna-tried-to-replace-its-workforce-with-ai

## Salesforce — Zero New SWE Hires (2025)
- CEO Marc Benioff: AI boosted engineering productivity >30%, so zero new software engineer hires in 2025
- Subsequently laid off ~4,000 customer support workers
- Stated AI handles up to half the company's work

## Amazon — The Largest Robotics Deployment
- 1M+ robots, nearly equal to human warehouse workforce
- 250,000 new robots added in 2024–2025 alone
- Packages per worker: 175 (2016) → 3,870 (2025) — 22x productivity gain
- Internal plans: avoid hiring 160,000+ people by 2027; 600,000 fewer total hires by 2033

**Source:** Fast Company | https://www.fastcompany.com/91514112/what-will-the-robot-jobs-apocalypse-look-like-ask-amazon-warehouse-workers

## Legal Profession — Support Displaced, Not Lawyers
- AI use doubled (14%→26%) 2023–2024; large firms at 47.8%
- But: 93.4% of 2024 law grads employed (record high)
- Lawyer employment hit record 1,208,100 in December 2025
- What's displaced: legal SUPPORT staff (research, secretarial, know-how)
- GPT-4-class models score top 10% on bar exam but adoption lags capability

---

## Radiology — The Case That Defied Predictions
- BLS projects 9% GROWTH for radiologists through 2034
- Record 1,208 residency positions in 2025 (up 4%)
- UK: 33% radiology workforce shortfall — driving DEMAND upward
- Only ~30% of radiologists use AI in routine clinical workflows
- AI is triaging and enhancing images, not reading them independently

**Lesson:** Technical capability ≠ deployment. Economic, regulatory, and quality factors delay even technically ready AI systems.

## Scale of 2025–2026 AI-Attributed Layoffs
- 2024: 12,700 confirmed AI-attributed US job losses
- 2025: ~55,000 job cuts directly attributed to AI (Challenger, Gray & Christmas)
- Q1 2026: ~52,000 tech layoffs; ~47.9% attributed to AI
- Tech unemployment: 5.8% (early 2026) — highest since dot-com bust

---

## Important Caveat
BLS aggregate employment data still looks healthy. Some companies use "AI" as a good excuse for layoffs driven by other factors (over-hiring corrections, macroeconomic). The actual AI-attributable fraction may be overstated in corporate narratives.
