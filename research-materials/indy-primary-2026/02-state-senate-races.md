# Indiana State Senate Races — Indianapolis May 2026 Primary
*Research compiled for voter guide. All citations in main guide.*

## Overview

Three Senate districts in or touching Indianapolis have contested primaries in 2026:

- **SD-29**: Covers westside Indianapolis, Pike Township, parts of Carmel/Zionsville. OPEN (Ford running for Congress). Both parties have contested primaries.
- **SD-31**: Covers Fishers (Hamilton County) + parts of northern Lawrence Township/Geist area. OPEN (Walker not seeking re-election). Both parties have contested primaries. Possible flip district.
- **SD-46**: Covers downtown Indianapolis, Near Eastside, Irvington, Martindale-Brightwood, Fountain Square. OPEN (Hunley running for mayor 2027). NO Republican filed. Democratic primary = the election.

### REPUBLICAN PRIMARY — SD-29

#### Mike Delph
- Former State Senator SD-29, 2005-2018. Attorney. Music recording/publishing businesses. USAR Lt. Colonel. Former aide to Spartz and Dan Burton.
- **Lost his seat to J.D. Ford in 2018 by 13+ percentage points** — the district's biggest recent electoral upset
- **Data Centers:** STRONGEST anti-data-center position in SD-29 Republican field. Would impose moratorium as FIRST ACT as senator. Calls data center expansion "out of control" and "driving energy affordability into the stratosphere."
- **Housing/Cost of Living:** Supports aggressive property tax relief
- **Endorsements:** Gov. Mike Braun, AG Todd Rokita, conservative attorney Jim Bopp, Republican Steelworkers PAC
- **Controversies:**
  - 2014: After Republican caucus declined to advance same-sex marriage constitutional amendment, Delph posted 150+ tweets in a single night attacking Republican leadership as weak, calling out "Godless" gay marriage supporters, and blasting ministers who didn't condemn homosexuality. The caucus stripped him of multiple leadership positions the next day.
  - His brother Stephen is gay; Stephen publicly disagreed with Mike but said the family has been supportive.
  - Deeply anti-LGBTQ+ rights record throughout legislative career.
  - 13-point loss to Ford in 2018 suggests he is not a strong general election candidate.

#### V. Roni Ford (Veronica Ford)
- Lifelong Hoosier; Wayne Township. Former Pike Township School Board member (2016-2020). Former deputy director of minority outreach, U.S. Sen. Todd Young. Director of Access to Recovery (SAMHSA-funded program). 10+ years Allstate. Indiana Black Expo involvement.
- **Housing/Cost of Living:** "Focus on practical solutions that support small businesses, create jobs and help families manage rising costs." Described as "real estate advisor focused on expanding housing opportunities and addressing homelessness" — strongest housing framing in SD-29 Republican field.
- **Data Centers:** No specific position found
- **Controversies:** None
- **Profile:** Most moderate Republican in the SD-29 field

#### John Ruckelshaus
- Former state rep (1990s) and state senator SD-30 (2016-2020, lost re-election). VP, Government Affairs, Indiana State Medical Association. Former Deputy Commissioner, Dept. of Workforce Development (Daniels administration).
- **Housing/Cost of Living:** After canvassing 12,500+ doors: heard consistent affordability and crime concerns. Supports long-term property tax relief protecting local governments and schools from unintended consequences. States 2026 state officials "pursued growth and infrastructure without fully respecting local planning and affordability."
- **Data Centers/LEAP:** Supports creating a "state water czar" to protect Eagle Creek. Pledges to lower utility costs and hold LEAP stakeholders to promises. Does NOT take a moratorium position.
- **Endorsements:** Former Gov. Mitch Daniels, Indiana Chamber of Commerce, OneZone Business Action, Fraternal Order of Police, Professional Firefighters
- **Controversies:** None

## Senate District 31

### DEMOCRATIC PRIMARY

#### Andrew Dezelan
- Employee benefits broker/consultant. Fishers resident. Former policy director, Indiana Senate Democratic Caucus (2014-2025).
- **Housing/Cost of Living:** Emphasizes child care affordability crisis
- **Data Centers:** No specific position found
- **MAJOR ACCOUNTABILITY ISSUE:** On April 26, 2026 — **six days before the primary** — Dezelan was arrested while canvassing in Fishers. Police found a small plastic bag of suspected cocaine in his vehicle. Faces preliminary charges of cocaine possession and resisting law enforcement. He is remaining in the race; charges are pending. Indiana law does not disqualify candidates based on arrest alone.

#### Kerry Forestal
- Current Marion County Sheriff (elected 2018, re-elected 2022). First joined sheriff's dept. 1977. U.S. Marshal, Southern District of Indiana (Obama nominee, 2010-2015). Term-limited as sheriff.
- **Housing/Cost of Living:** First bill would target Indiana's preemption statutes — laws that strip cities from governing themselves, including setting their own housing policies. Central to housing advocates' concerns.
- **Data Centers:** No specific position found
- **Sheriff's Record (accountability items):**
  - Jail overcrowding above 2,400-inmate cap; ICE detainees and DOC transfers contribute
  - 29 in-custody deaths at jail 2021-2024 (per federal lawsuits)
  - Notable deaths: Edward Townsend (2022, shot by deputy), Dennis Di Genova (2023, ruled homicide after alleged intake use-of-force)
  - Successfully sued Indiana Law Enforcement Training Board for refusing to train MCSO deputies
  - Opposes housing ICE detainees beyond short-term holds
  - No personal corruption or ethical violations found
- **Campaign Finance:** No specific totals found

#### Lasima O. Packett
- Indiana Army National Guard, 20 years (1998-2018). Deployments: Iraq, Afghanistan, Guantanamo. Obama White House intern. Mayor's Neighborhood Advocate (Indianapolis). Pursuing master's in urban design.
- **Housing/Cost of Living:** Affordable housing, property tax caps for seniors/veterans/fixed income. Wants tenant protections and workforce housing expansion.
- **Data Centers:** No specific position found
- **Campaign Finance:** No data found
- **Controversies:** None

#### Catherine Torzewski
- Real estate agent; small business investor. Fishers. Was campaign manager for Rep. Victoria Garcia Wilburn's 2024 re-election.
- **Housing/Cost of Living:** "Policies that lower everyday costs"
- **Data Centers:** No specific position found
- **Campaign Finance:** No data found
- **Controversies:** None

## Senate District 46

**NO REPUBLICAN FILED. Democratic primary winner IS the next senator.**
Covers: downtown Indianapolis, Near Eastside, Irvington, Martindale-Brightwood, Fountain Square, Fletcher Place, Beech Grove, parts of south side.

### Sam Glynn
- Age 25. First-time candidate. Political science degree, Marian University. Staffer, Marion County Treasurer's Office.
- **Data Centers:** Supports BANNING new data centers. "These data centers discourage development because that land could be used for parks, recreation centers, or high-density housing." Also argues data centers increase coal dependence and electricity bills.
- **Housing/Cost of Living:** Minimum wage increase to $15.88 tied to legislative pay increases. Expand public transportation. Walkable cities.
- **Accountability/Transparency:** Opposes corporate and out-of-state campaign donations. Supports term limits and age limits (no official over 67; no legislator serving 20+ years).
- **Campaign Finance:** Small-dollar, local focus by design
- **Concerns:** No prior elected experience. 25 years old. First-time candidate in a competitive primary.

### Allissa Impink
- Director of statewide community engagement, Women's Foundation of Indiana. Former child welfare case manager. **IPS School Board Commissioner (District 4, elected 2024)**. Fletcher Place neighborhood resident.
- **Data Centers:** "Data centers need to demonstrate their benefits to our community — and they have not done that." Supports **moratorium while effective ordinances are developed.**
- **Housing/Cost of Living:** Expanding affordable housing. Opposes criminalizing poverty. Expanding child care.
- **Accountability:** Active on IPS board; education advocacy and nonprofit community ties.
- **Campaign Finance:** Nonprofit/education networks likely donor base
- **Controversies:** None

### Clif Marsiglio
- Project manager, Indiana University. Near East Side Community Organization board member. 30 years as IU educator/administrator. Has run unsuccessfully for: LG (2024), Indianapolis mayor (2023), Indiana House HD-100 (2020). Regularly present at City-County Council and Statehouse events. Has publicly protested Mayor Hogsett.
- **Data Centers:** **Worked directly with Martindale-Brightwood neighbors to oppose the Metrobloks data center** — the most hands-on opposition experience in the SD-46 field. Opposes corporate subsidies for data centers.
- **Housing/Cost of Living:** Expand affordable housing. Strengthen renter protections to prevent displacement. Opposes corporate tax breaks draining local resources.
- **Accountability:** Anti-establishment; visible opposition to Democratic machine (Hogsett protests); opposes corporate subsidies
- **Campaign Finance:** Progressive base, small-dollar
- **Concerns:** Three prior losses at different levels. Long-time activist who has not won office.
