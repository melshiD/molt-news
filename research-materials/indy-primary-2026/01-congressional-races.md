# Congressional Races — Indianapolis May 2026 Primary
*Research compiled for voter guide. All citations in main guide.*

## Overview

Three congressional districts touch Marion County/Indianapolis:
- **7th CD**: Most of Indianapolis/Marion County (D+heavy; Democratic primary IS the election)
- **6th CD**: Southern/southeastern Marion County + rural southern Indiana (R+heavy; Republican primary IS the election)
- **5th CD**: Northern Indianapolis suburbs + Carmel/Fishers areas (R-leaning but competitive suburban)

**Indiana's primaries are OPEN** — voters declare party when requesting a ballot. No pre-registration required.

## Indiana 7th Congressional District

### André Carson (D, Incumbent) — 18 years in office

**Background:** 51 years old. Elected 2008 in special election succeeding his grandmother Julia Carson. One of four Muslim members of Congress.

**Effectiveness Rating:** Ranked 197th/220 House Democrats by Center for Effective Lawmaking (joint UVA/Vanderbilt project) in the 118th Congress — bottom 10%.

**Campaign Finance:**
- $646,000 cash on hand (end 2025)
- Accepts corporate PAC money
- AES Indiana PAC donation received
- **BlackRock PAC: $5,000 received September 30, 2025** — ONE DAY before AES Corp's $33B acquisition by BlackRock-led consortium was announced publicly
- Accepted additional AES check December 2025, during acquisition process
- Also accepted Google PAC donations
- Did not publicly comment on AES/BlackRock acquisition until February 2026 — two months after deal closed

**Data Centers:**
- Silent when Martindale-Brightwood neighborhood fought proposed $500M data center (over 100 residents at City Hall)
- City approved data center April 1, 2026 despite opposition
- Now claims to support federal data center moratorium — position adopted only after challengers made it a campaign issue
- Credibility severely undermined by AES/BlackRock PAC timing

**Housing:**
- Rhetorical support for affordable housing and Fair Housing Center
- District has some of highest eviction rates in the country despite his 18-year tenure
- Challengers note this happened "on his watch"

**Accountability Summary:**
- AES/BlackRock PAC timing is the central concern — accepted money from utility company being acquired by private equity one day before announcement
- No formal ethics complaints or investigations found
- Low legislative effectiveness despite claiming billions for Indiana

### Destiny Wells (D, Challenger)

**Background:** Age 42. U.S. Army Reserve Lt. Colonel. Law degree (University of Texas). Former Indiana Deputy AG. 2024 Democratic AG nominee. Entered race Feb 6, 2026.

**Why Running:** "Stagnancy in Indiana Democratic politics contributing to apathy and low voter turnout." Has called Carson "a corporate Democrat" and says party insiders discourage candidates from discussing his corporate money.

**Data Centers:**
- Strongly supports **AI Data Center Moratorium Act** (Sanders/AOC federal bill)
- Lives near neighborhoods affected by data center proposals
- Frames as corporate power vs. community safety issue

**Housing:**
- Links private equity (BlackRock specifically) to housing crisis
- States: "when we allow private equity portfolios like BlackRock...to come in and buy all this housing up, it does raise the prices"
- Connects housing affordability directly to crime

**Cost of Living:** Prioritizes lowering costs for families including healthcare, housing, and utilities. Supports Break Up Big Medicine Act.

**Campaign Finance:** 
- NO corporate PAC money
- $15,000 transferred from state campaign account to new PAC "The Good Fight"
- Limited fundraising total disclosed

**Accountability:** Anti-corruption is the core of her campaign. Has spoken publicly about AES/BlackRock donation timing.

### George Hornedo (D, Challenger)

**Background:** Age 35. Degrees from Cornell, Harvard, and GWU Law. Attorney. Obama Administration DOJ. Obama Foundation policing reform. Pete Buttigieg 2020 campaign. Indiana Latino Democratic Caucus member. Has been running for ~1 year; knocked 35,000+ doors.

**Why Running:** Criticizes "18 years of the same representation"; cites Carson's 197th/220 effectiveness ranking.

**Data Centers:** Supports federal moratorium. Cites rising utility bills and environmental pollution.

**Housing:**
- Proposes **publicly owned land development** for affordable housing
- Proposes **housing trust fund**
- "Homelessness is a moral and policy failure"

**Cost of Living:**
- Cites Surface Transportation formula penalizing "blue cities in red states"
- Led federal outreach for ClimateReady Tech Hub ($19.5M in climate resilience funding)
- Proposes in-house grant support for agencies navigating federal applications

**Campaign Finance:**
- Raised $200,000+ (including $55,000 in self-loans)
- NO corporate PAC money

### Denise Paul Hatch (D, Challenger)

**Background:** Age 66. Former Center Township Constable. **DISQUALIFYING FACTOR:** Pleaded guilty October 2024 to: (1) felony official misconduct; (2) misdemeanor disorderly conduct; (3) misdemeanor unlawful carrying of a handgun. Removed from constable office by law. Currently appealing conviction.

**Policy Positions:**
- Opposes data centers and AES Corporation
- Supports rent control, minimum wage increase
- Universal Medicaid for pregnant women

**Accountability:** Her felony conviction for official misconduct WHILE holding public office is fundamentally disqualifying for a voter who prioritizes anti-corruption. She was literally removed from office for misconduct. Her stated policy positions otherwise align with voter priorities — but this disqualifies her.

## Indiana 6th Congressional District

### Jefferson Shreve (R, Incumbent)

**Background:** Age 60. Founded Storage Express (self-storage), sold to Extra Space Storage for ~$590M in 2022. Net worth ~$599M+. Lost 2023 Indianapolis mayoral race 40.5% to Hogsett's 59.5%. Won 2024 Republican primary with 28.4% in crowded field; won general 63.9%.

**Committee Assignments:** Transportation and Infrastructure; Foreign Affairs; **Appropriations** (significant seat)

**MAJOR ACCOUNTABILITY ISSUE — Stock Trading:**
- Jan-April 2025: 320+ stock transactions in first four months in Congress
- April 7-17, 2025: **140+ trades worth $3.44-$9.45 million** in 10-day window AFTER Trump's "Liberation Day" tariff announcement April 2
- Multiple trades in companies directly overseen by his own Transportation Committee: Uber (bought, +34%), Schneider National (bought, +16%), CSX (bought, +9%), UPS (bought); GE Aerospace, Norfolk Southern, Harley-Davidson, Carnival (sold)
- Non-transportation trades: Microsoft ($250-500K), Meta ($115-300K), Apple ($100-250K)
- Refused to identify his financial advisor or say whether he supports stock trading ban
- **Only stopped individual stock trading in June 2025 AFTER press exposure** — not voluntarily
- Watchdog: "We don't know what he knew and when he knew it or what he told his financial adviser"

**Key Vote:** Voted YES on One Big Beautiful Bill (Medicaid cuts, SNAP cuts, $3.3T deficit increase over decade)

**Data Centers:** No documented position. Unlikely to oppose given pro-business record.

**Housing:** No affordable housing legislation. Voted for bill cutting programs supporting low-income households.

**2026 Primary Challenger:** Sarah Janisse Brown (author/entrepreneur; parental rights, children's health focus — not aligned with this voter's priorities)

## Indiana 5th Congressional District

### Victoria Spartz (R, Incumbent)

**Background:** Age 47. Ukraine-born (Chernihiv). CPA. Carmel, Indiana. First elected 2020. Seeking 4th term.

**MAJOR ACCOUNTABILITY ISSUES (multiple):**
1. **Staff abuse/ethics probe:** House Ethics Committee opened inquiry 2024 into "abuse," "rage," "general toxicity." Staff turnover rate 3.5x House average. Ethics inquiry dropped November 2024 without full investigation — not a finding of innocence, just dropped.
2. **Weapons charge:** Charged July 2024 for carrying unloaded handgun through Dulles Airport security. Ethics Committee took no action.
3. **STOCK Act violation:** Failed to timely disclose Simon Property Group stock purchase worth $15K-$50K in 2021. No fine levied.
4. **Committee boycott:** Dec 2024 announced she would "not sit on committees or participate in the caucus" — reportedly after being denied Ways and Means Committee seat. This left her constituents without representation through committee work for months.

**Ukraine Inconsistency:**
- Was most vocal early advocate for Ukraine after 2022 invasion; visited Bucha and Lviv
- **April 2024: Voted AGAINST $60B Ukraine aid package — THREE DAYS after Russian missile strike on her hometown Chernihiv killed 18 people and injured 78**

**One Big Beautiful Bill:** Initially holdout, then voted YES after reported Trump pressure. More than 1 in 8 Hamilton County residents depends on a Medicaid program that this bill cut by 12%.

**Data Centers:** No documented position. No reason to expect opposition.

**Overall:** Multiple credibility and accountability issues. She votes against the core interests of her own district when politically convenient.

---

### J.D. Ford (D, Challenger — 5th District)

**Background:** Age 43. Carmel. Indiana State Senator (District 29) since 2018 — first openly LGBT person elected to Indiana General Assembly. Announced Congress run January 14, 2026. Motivated by redistricting fight (Trump/Vance attempted mid-decade redistricting to dilute Indianapolis's Democratic votes; Ford was part of the successful effort to defeat it).

**Housing/Cost of Living:**
- "The cost of groceries, housing, energy, and healthcare have exploded"
- Pledges to lower grocery and housing costs
- Proposes **strict clawback rules for corporate subsidies**
- Wants to cap out-of-pocket healthcare expenses

**Data Centers:** No documented specific position found.

**Accountability:** Redistricting fight shows willingness to stand up to power. Corporate subsidy clawback proposal is meaningful.

**Campaign Finance:** Well-known state senator; expected significant fundraising but specific 2026 figures not yet public.

**Note:** 5th District leans Republican. Ford is the best (only serious) Democratic option; winning the general would be an upset.
